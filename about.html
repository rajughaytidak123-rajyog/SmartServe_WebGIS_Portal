About SmartServe GIS

An enterprise-grade location-based multi-service WebGIS portal and cloud-native spatial infrastructure platform for Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad.

Project Background & Study Area

SmartServe GIS was developed to provide citizens, urban planners, and municipal administrators with an interactive, multi-tier spatial decision-support platform. The application covers two adjoining municipal corporations in Maharashtra:

  • Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC): Primary study area of 251.02 km², hosting 3,541 services and the focal area for pedestrian network accessibility analysis.
  • Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC): Secondary study area of 176.74 km², hosting 1,206 services. Both municipalities are retained with full boundary and POI integrity.

The system indexes 4,747 verified Points of Interest (POIs) across 9 essential domains: Healthcare (1,081), Food (1,217), Finance (758), Education (444), Recreation (507), Transport (334), Shopping (195), Accommodation (128), and Emergency (74).

Scientific Methodology & Mathematical Formulas

1. Metric Projected Coordinate System (UTM Zone 43N)

Geographic decimal degrees in WGS 84 (EPSG:4326) shrink with latitude ($\Delta \text{Lon} = 111.32 \times \cos(\text{lat})\text{ km}$). Applying Euclidean formulas directly yields severe distortions. All metric lengths, areas, and network calculations are computed in WGS 84 / UTM Zone 43N (EPSG:32643) with minimal scale distortion (< 0.04%).

2. Pedestrian Network Isochrones vs. Euclidean Circular Buffers

Pedestrians cannot walk in straight lines through buildings, rivers, and walled compounds. They are constrained by Pune's 7,163 km walkable street graph. Multi-source Dijkstra routing traverses the actual network graph at 4.8 km/h (normal) and 3.6 km/h (slower).

Path Efficiency = (Network Isochrone Area / Euclidean Buffer Area) × 100%
Result in Pune: 62.1% Efficiency (Buffer overestimates area by ~38% to 41%)

3. Demographic Vulnerability Gap

The vulnerability gap measures residents who have 15-minute healthcare access under normal adult walking conditions (4.8 km/h) but lose access when walking at a slower, realistic pace (3.6 km/h) due to age, pregnancy, young children, or physical disability.

Vulnerability Gap = Reachable_Pop(4.8 km/h) − Reachable_Pop(3.6 km/h)
Result in Pune: 3,714,728 − 3,604,072 = 110,656 Residents (2.77% of PMC)
